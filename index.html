    How to link it

    One point per line: ID, local easting and northing (same column order as your point file), then easting and northing in . Use 3 or more points to get a residual check.

    Shift only keeps the rotation and scale from Parameters and averages the readings. It suits phone GPS best. The full fit needs 3 or more points far apart.

    Rotation is the grid bearing of the local north axis, clockwise from grid north. Scale 0 ppm means no scale change.

    Indoor tracking

    Adjusts itself each time you tap "I'm at" on a known point after walking 8 m or more. Hold the phone in front of you, screen up, while walking.

    Export

    CSV with ID, easting and northing in the coordinate system above, plus WGS84 lat/lon and GPS accuracy.

    Share with the team

    One file with the points, the coordinate settings and the calibration. Teammates open it with "Import site setup or point file" and are ready to go. Share it only through company channels, since it contains the site coordinates.
